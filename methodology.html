METHODOLOGY

From official document to dependable dataset.

Data Mine separates reported values from calculated indicators and keeps every release connected to its source.

Publication control

No extracted dataset becomes public until it has been reviewed and approved by Jochebed.

01

Extract

Tables are taken from official publications while preserving labels, units, time periods, notes and page references.

02

Validate

Year coverage, totals, percentages, missing values and unit consistency are checked against the original document.

03

Interpret

Shares, annual changes and long-run growth rates are calculated without altering the underlying reported record. Interpretations are written in plain language and separated from source facts.

04

Approve and publish

Only approved releases appear in the public dashboard and data library. Each release retains a clear provenance statement.
