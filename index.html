THE BIG PICTURE

A growing system in the middle of a transition

Ghana is using more energy, but the sources supplying it have changed significantly. The national mix has moved away from biomass dominance towards a more diversified system led by oil, natural gas and biomass.

2000
Biomass defined the system

Two-thirds of total energy supply came from biomass. Oil supplied one-quarter, while natural gas and solar were absent from the reported mix.

2010s
Natural gas entered at scale

Gas grew rapidly during the decade, changing the structure of supply and strengthening its role in Ghana’s modern energy system.

2024
Growth brought a more balanced mix

Total supply reached 13,741 ktoe. Oil, gas and biomass each held substantial shares, while solar remained below 1% of reported supply.

INVESTMENT SIGNAL

Demand growth is clear. The opportunity is in how future supply is met.

Total energy supply increased by 106% between 2000 and 2024. Solar’s reported contribution remains very small, signalling room for deeper investigation into renewable generation, grid readiness, financing and project pipelines.
