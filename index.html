GHANA’S ENERGY STORY

A national system transformed in one generation.

In 2000, biomass supplied two-thirds of Ghana’s energy. By 2024, the system had more than doubled and natural gas had become a major source.

The transition is not a simple replacement story. Demand grew, new fuels entered and the balance of the system changed.
